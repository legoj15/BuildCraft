###### Changes since 2026.2.0-br2:
- A robot's icon now shows its charge at a glance — no more mousing over to tell a full robot from an empty one. The eyes use 7.1.x's own overlay art and behaviour, which the inventory icon never had (upstream hardcoded the icon to look fully charged): they now fade from dark at 0% to bright red at full, exactly like a robot's eyes in the world always have. The classic pale-cyan charging port on the robot's underside is drawn too, always on, as upstream drew it. Both show in inventories, hands, item frames, and on dropped stacks. Robots also show the standard durability-style charge bar under their icon (empty shows the bare trough, full draws all 13 px), in the classic readout colours — dark red empty through green full — the same colours the charge tooltip text has always used. The empty-board template robot shows none of it — no charge tooltip, no bar, and dark eyes — because a blank chassis with no program is not a functional robot and holds nothing worth reading; classic BuildCraft hid the charge line on it too.
- Robots in your inventory, on the ground, and in item frames are drawn at their classic size again — they had been rendering far smaller than classic BuildCraft showed them. Robots held in hand and robots in the world are unchanged.
- Minecraft 26.2: BuildCraft now shows its square gear icon in the mods list (the revamped screen expects one and previously showed none), and its classic logo as the wide banner in the mod details. The new properties are declared alongside the old one, so older Minecraft versions are unchanged.
- Fixed the "BuildCraft Robots" tab's icon: it was a data-less board stack, which rendered as the wrong blank PCB (the 1.12.2-era art) instead of the classic Empty Board chip — the same reason a plain `/give`d board looked different from the creative-tab one. The tab now shows a proper Empty Board, and boards with no or unknown board data render as the Empty Board on every Minecraft version, exactly as classic BuildCraft's registry fallback did — so there is only one Empty Board look again.

- Every robot in the "BuildCraft Robots" tab wears its own colour again: each ready-made robot shows the per-kind skin it wears in the world (blue picker, red knight, and so on) instead of all stacks drawing as the same white chassis, and the empty board's clean chip is distinct again. On Minecraft 1.21.1 the redstone boards also show their tier colours (green/blue/red/clean) like they already do on the newer Minecraft versions.

- The Robot Station is called "Docking Station" again, matching classic BuildCraft. Only the name changed — the item, its recipe, and existing worlds are unaffected.
- Robots have their own creative tab again, like classic BuildCraft: "BuildCraft Robots" holds the zone planner, every ready-made robot, the robot station, and every redstone board — the robot station no longer hides in the Pluggables tab.
- Minecraft 1.21.1: fragile fluid shards and all BuildCraft buckets (oil and fuel variants) were drawn far too large whenever held, dropped, worn, or shown in an item frame — they now render at the standard item size like they do on the other Minecraft versions.
- The Tank robot program is in: a Tank Board robot is the fluid carrier — it loads fluid at a supply station (a robot station on a wooden fluid pipe pointed at a tank), hauls up to four buckets in its internal tank, and unloads into the pipe network at another station. Like the item Carrier it needs a Provide Fluids gate on the supply station (and an Accept Fluids gate to unload); a "Filter"-style work filter holding a filled bucket narrows what it will load. It joins the other boards in the creative tab with its green chip and its own robot skin. It also uncovered a real robot bug: a robot's tank could gain (or lose) fluid during a station search's dry run instead of only when actually loading or unloading — searches are now purely observational.
- New config option `disableFacadeGeneration` (Common → general): turns BuildCraft's automatic facade generation completely off — no startup block scan, no generated assembly recipes, no facade variants in the creative tab, no JEI entries, and no client-side texture-dedup scan. Meant for players using another universal-facades mod. Facades already placed in a world keep their looks, and the (now empty) BuildCraft Facades tab stays registered; takes effect after a restart. (#29)
- Robot station gates are functional: all 22 robot/station triggers and actions from classic BuildCraft work again — the sleep trigger and wake-up action, the in-station and linked triggers, go-to-station and work-in-area actions with map-location zones, and the item/fluid filters that decide what a robot may take from or leave at a station. Gates now also govern robot behaviour the way they always should have: a station with no gate refuses robot item and fluid transfers instead of letting any robot use it freely, so a station you want a robot to use needs an Accept/Provide Items (or fluids) gate.
- Minecraft 1.21.10/1.21.11: the Planter robot no longer recognises seeds there (a version-cliff bug since the boards shipped) — it plants crops on those versions like it does on 26.x.
- Minecraft 1.21.1: heat-exchanger output slots cap at one bucket again — a hardcoded slot limit of 64 let a click or shift-click stack a whole bucket pile into a single-bucket slot.
- Eight more robot programs: Lumberjack (breaks logs), Miner (mines ores, and only what its tool can actually mine), Harvester (harvests mature crops), Planter (plants seeds), Farmer (hoes dirt for crops), Pump (moves fluids between the robot's tank and its station), Knight (fights mobs), and Butcher (hunts animals). They join Picker and Carrier in the creative tab — each as its own board with a tier-colored chip (green Picker/Carrier, blue work boards, red Knight) and as ready-made robots.
- Fixed: animals no longer float up out of crude oil — they sink and stay put like they should, since oil is too thick to swim in. The fix now reaches Minecraft 26.1.2 too, where it hadn't before. (On 26.1.2 the same change also means oil pools no longer cushion falls; oil isn't water.)
- Falling into crude oil now breaks your fall on Minecraft 26.2 — drop into an oil pool from any height and you land unharmed, like into water. Oil currents also push you along in shallow oil now, not only when you're fully submerged.
- Robots finally have work: the first two robot programs (Picker and Carrier boards) are in. A Picker robot flies around picking up dropped items within 250 blocks and unloads them at a station; a Carrier robot loads cargo from a supply station (a robot station on a wooden pipe) and unloads it into the pipe network there — carrying it to a *different* station arrives with the gate actions in a later phase. An un-programmed (empty) robot now flies home to its station and parks instead of idling in place. Robots also recharge themselves now: a robot that runs low flies to any power-providing station on its own and tops itself up. (Boards are creative-tab only for now — the programming table that makes them in survival arrives later.)
- Robots are back — the first working piece of them. A Robot itself now exists: place the (creative-only, for now) Robot item on a free Robot Station and it docks there, charges from the pipe network, and shows its charge as a glowing overlay. Robots have no work programs yet — those come next — so a placed robot idles at its station. Damage drains a robot's charge instead of health; a robot that runs out is destroyed and drops itself (keeping its remaining charge) along with anything it was carrying. Sneak-click with a wrench to peel a robot off its station and back into your inventory.
- The Robot Station can now be crafted (3 iron ingots + 1 Gold Chipset) — it was accidentally creative-only when it shipped.
- Robot Stations look like they used to again: the classic two-part pedestal — a plate against the pipe with a short post standing out from it — instead of the plain stub they had been. Their status colour now reads from any angle, not just head-on: green for a free station, yellow once a robot has claimed one, red for a robot's home station. The station's outline also grew to match, so it's easier to click.
- For addon authors: the robot API changed shape while it was still unused — robots are plain entities rather than living ones, AIs program against the new `IRobotAccess` interface, a robot's maximum charge doubled to match the classic 1 MJ = 10 RF conversion, and 7.1.x's backwards `isActive()` (which returned true for a *sleeping* robot) is now honestly named `isSleeping()`.
- The mod jar now includes its license texts (`META-INF/LICENSE` and `META-INF/LICENSE.API`). The jar previously shipped with none, even though parts of the BuildCraft API it contains are distributed under the MIT license, which requires the notice to travel with the code.
- Added the Robot Station pluggable: place it on a pipe to create a docking station (`buildcraftunofficial:robot_station`). A docked robot charges directly from the pipe network; the station's indicator shows available/reserved/linked. (First piece of the in-progress robotics restoration — no robots to dock yet.)
- Spectators can now view Assembly Table, Advanced Crafting Table, and Integration Table interfaces
- Added a complete Simplified Chinese (`zh_cn`) translation (AI/machine assisted), thank you [beizhou1](https://github.com/beizhou1)!

- A large amount of text that was hardcoded in English now goes through the language files
- The Filler and Filler Planner now explain their pattern slot and pattern parameters in the help ledger — previously the two most important controls on those screens were the only ones with no help. The Filler Planner also regained its help ledger, which had been switched off by mistake.
- With RF unit names enabled, the FE Engine's upgrade help, the FE limiter pipe's gate actions, and the guide book's pipe chapter now say "RF" like the rest of the interface instead of remaining "FE".
- The Replacer's help now states that the single-block schematics are not consumed, which differs from older BuildCraft versions.
- The help ledger shows a warning icon when a screen has no help available, instead of opening an empty panel that looked identical to a working one.
- The Fragile Fluid Container's contents now follow your unit display settings rather than always showing raw millibuckets.
- Removed the non-functional Guide Note item (a creative-only stub that never opened anything, in this port or 1.12.2).
