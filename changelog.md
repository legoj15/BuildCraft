###### Changes since 2026.3.0:

- Fixed a rare crash when chunks holding Timers, Light Sensors, Pulsars, Lenses or Filters loaded. Lenses and Filters also pick up resource-pack texture changes after a reload now. Resource packs can now reshape Timers, Light Sensors and Pulsars (`models/plugs/timer.json`, `light_sensor.json`, `pulsar_static.json`) like the Blocker and Power Adapter.
