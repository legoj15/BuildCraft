###### Changes since 2026.2.1:

- Fuel temperature now matters in the Combustion Engine: hot fuel gives 25% more power than cool, searing fuel 25% less and runs the engine hot enough to need extra coolant. Hot and searing fuels used to be refused outright.
- Oil now spawns in the Nether, always searing: slicks on the lava sea, buried pockets and small spouts, with rare infinite springs in Crimson Forests. Existing config files still list the Nether as excluded; remove it from `excludedDimensions` to turn it on.
- Oils and fuels burn, lighter ones faster: fire catches a pool of crude or heavy oil slowly and it burns for a long time, while light fuels catch quickly and fire races across them, heating a fuel makes it burn faster, and residue never burns. Gaseous fuels explode when lit (by fire, lava or a lit campfire next to them), and in the Nether on their own; each blast sets off any gas close by a moment later. Burning and explosions can each be turned off in the config.
- A Pump on top of a Mining Well or a Quarry drains the lava or oil in the machine's way, drawing on the machine's power when its own runs out. The well digs on, and a finished Quarry goes back for the blocks the liquid was covering. The pump's status light turns blue to show the link.
- A redstone signal now pauses the Pump, Mining Well and Quarry, as their guide pages say: they keep their progress and spend no power, and carry on when the signal is removed. A paused Pump still pushes on what it already holds.
- Lasers work underwater: water no longer blocks a laser's line of sight, and a beam fired through water fizzes with bubbles.
- Lasers, laser tables, engines, tanks, chutes, distillers, heat exchangers and quarry frames can be waterlogged, so they no longer sit in a pocket of air when placed underwater.
- Pipes sound like what they're made of: wooden pipes creak like scaffolding, iron, gold and stripes pipes ring like copper grates, emzuli pipes chime like amethyst, stone, obsidian, void and structure pipes sound like stone, and sandstone and clay pipes each have their own. Diamond, wooden diamond, daizuli, lapis and quartz pipes keep the classic metal sound. Redstone engines now sound wooden, Stirling engines stony, markers like a higher-pitched torch and quarry frames like metal grates.
- Looking at a marker laser shows its length in blocks under the crosshair, including the faint preview lines while holding a Marker Wrench.
- The Filler has a Preview toggle (the eye button on its screen). On, it draws see-through cubes over every spot its pattern still has to fill, for everyone near it, and hides them while it is placing or breaking blocks. It replaces the Filler Planner: the Filler Planner item and the Volume Box addon spots are gone, so Filler Planners in inventories and on Volume Boxes disappear; Fillers that were driven by a Filler Planner in an older world take over the Volume Box, pattern and inverted setting the planner held, the first time each loads.
- Changing a Filler's pattern, parameters, invert toggle or box now reaches other players watching it straight away.
- The Zone Planner's map now shows terrain past your render distance: anywhere in its survey area that someone has visited is drawn from what the server remembers, without loading any chunks.
- Facades cast soft corner shadows like the blocks they copy, so a wall or floor of facades blends with real blocks instead of each facade looking flat-lit. A pipe with any solid facade shades the blocks on all its sides, like a full block would; hollow facades cast none.
- Wrenches come in three tiers. The Wrench is now the Basic Wrench. The Marker Connector is now the Marker Wrench and also works as a wrench; aiming at a marker laser or a volume box still links or edits it instead of turning the block behind it. The new Volume Wrench (diamond chipset, diamond gear and a Marker Wrench) can drag the corners of any volume box.
- A marker volume can be resized: right-click one of its corner markers with the Marker Wrench, drag, and right-click to confirm. The markers drop and a floating box replaces them, which the next Quarry, Filler or Architect Table placed against it takes the same way it takes markers. Sneak-right-click cancels, or throws away an unused box.
- A Filler or Architect Table placed against connected survival markers, or against a floating box lifted off them, now keeps that area as a volume box: the Volume Wrench can resize it (no further than markers could reach on each side, or the box's own width if it is already wider), resizing restarts the machine's job, and the box disappears when the machine is broken. Boxes from the creative Volume Box stay as before.
- Quarries and Mining Wells can be enchanted at an anvil with Silk Touch, Fortune or Efficiency. Silk Touch and Fortune work on what the machine mines as they would on a pickaxe (Silk Touch keeps ores and stone whole, Fortune adds drops from ores and the like), and Efficiency cuts the power each block takes (Efficiency V mines on under half). The machines keep their enchantments, their shine and any name given at the anvil when broken. The `machineEnchantments` option turns enchanting them off.
- With GuideME (the guide mod Applied Energistics 2 uses) installed, the BuildCraft guide is also available as a GuideME book: GuideME's open-guide key (G by default) on a BuildCraft item's tooltip jumps to its page.
- Fluid pipes connect to cauldrons: a pipe fills one with water or lava a bucket at a time, and a wooden fluid pipe drains it. Item pipes likewise connect to composters.
- The Replacer can remove a block from a blueprint: leave the replacement slot empty and the Replace button becomes Remove, turning every match into air (handy for clearing grass tufts out of a captured build).
- Roughly Enough Items (REI) support is back on every Minecraft version and matches JEI: every BuildCraft machine's recipes, fuels and coolants, the "+" button that moves ingredients into a machine, clickable progress areas, looking up the fluid in a tank, and separate entries for each paintbrush colour, fluid shard, lens, gate, facade, board and robot.
- Fixed a Filler on Loop resending its full data to nearby players every tick after finishing a cycle.
- A Builder that has stopped getting on with its build now shows why: while you hold a Marker Wrench or Volume Wrench, it outlines every cell it is skipping (red for a block that cannot stand or be broken, or that protection refused; yellow for missing items; blue for fluid in the way), and hovering the "Left to Place" readout on its screen sums them up.
- Fixed a Builder (with excavation on) or Filler never placing anything while its build area held one unbreakable or protected block (bedrock, a claimed block).
- Wrenches are real tools now: the Basic and Marker Wrench are iron (250 uses, mended with iron ingots) and the Volume Wrench is diamond (1561 uses, mended with diamonds). Each thing you turn with one, each hit on a mob (they hit like a light tool) and each block you mine costs a use; linking markers and handling volume boxes cost none. They mine BuildCraft's machines, engines, tanks and pipes at iron or diamond speed, and can take Unbreaking, Mending, Efficiency, Smite and Bane of Arthropods. Rotating a wooden or iron pipe, clearing an overheated engine and dismantling a robot now count as wrenching (swing and the Wrenched advancement included). Some zombies, husks and zombie villagers carry a Basic Wrench, and a zombie killed by a player sometimes drops a worn one. The Programming Table and Power Tester now drop when broken with a pickaxe too; before, nothing counted as the right tool for them.
- The Advanced Crafting Table can swap materials. Its cycle-output button now also offers the same recipe in other woods and materials (oak planks in a stair pattern step through every wood's stairs) and picking one rewrites the blueprint to that material; the item tags that count are the new `craftingVariantTags` config option. A new button beside it lets the table accept substitute materials: with it on, the selected recipe can be crafted from any other material that still fits it (birch planks for an oak planks blueprint), preferring the blueprint's own items.
