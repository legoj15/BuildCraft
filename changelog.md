###### Changes since 2026.3.2:

- The `searingFluidSteam` option (searing fluids venting steam) moved from the common config to the client config (`buildcraftunofficial-client.toml`, under `display`), since it only changes what each player sees. A value set in the old place is not carried over; it starts back at its default (on).
- **Fabric support (in development) for every supported Minecraft version: 1.21.1, 1.21.10, 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3.** BuildCraft now also builds for Fabric (with Fabric API and Forge Config API Port); the NeoForge versions are unchanged. On 1.21.1 Fabric the fluid inside tanks and fluid containers is drawn flat in the inventory, and BuildCraft's chunk loaders do not keep their chunks loaded across a server restart. Machines respect the Fabric claim mods that answer through the bundled Common Protection API, placements included (a claim mod that guards placements only through its own mixins is not asked about a placement). Known Fabric differences so far: no Curios/Accessories slot for the Robot Goggles, and fluids move in whole millibuckets (a transfer of less than 1 mB moves nothing).
- Fixed boats sinking in crude oil, residue and the fuels on every version (gases excepted): they float on them as on water again, and with `oilIsSticky` on they sail at half speed there.
- Fixed mobs and players being stuck in place in crude oil and residue on Minecraft 26.1.2 and later: they now wade through slowly (residue the slowest, and slower again with `oilIsSticky`), sink gently, and can climb out at the edge.
- Fixed a blank facade (one with no block, e.g. from `/give`) placing onto a pipe as an invisible cover; it is now refused, and no placement outline is shown for it. Likewise, a lens no longer shows a placement outline on a pipe that is not an item pipe, nor a power adaptor or pulsar on a pipe engines cannot power, since none of them can be placed there.
- Fixed facades of many blocks (double slabs, waterlogged variants and other look-alikes of a block that already has a facade) showing as invisible, and named "air", for players on a dedicated server, and in single-player turning into empty facades when such a facade arrived from elsewhere (a server world, `/give`). Facades hidden as look-alikes also come back to the creative tab, recipe viewer and Assembly Table when a resource pack change makes them look different (from the next world join), instead of only after a restart.
- Fixed two Fabric network gaps in the in-development build: a very large blueprint or template sent to a player no longer fails to send, and a player whose BuildCraft network protocol differs from the server's is now turned away at login with a message naming the mismatch, instead of joining and misreading machine updates, and a player without BuildCraft is turned away with a message asking for it.
- Fixed a living entity's hands and armour reading as one unnamed slot to BuildCraft's machines on Fabric, instead of the six indexed slots NeoForge's entity item capability hands out.
- The Fabric build now asks only for the oldest Fabric Loader (0.19.4) and Fabric API (0.160.6) it has been tested on, instead of the newest builds at the time it was made.
- Fixed every BuildCraft chunk loader silently stopping after switching worlds on Fabric: the chunk tickets' owner bookkeeping was never cleared when a world unloaded, so the previous world's records told the new one its chunks were already ticketed.
- Fixed more Fabric behaviour in the in-development build: claim mods are asked before a wrench turns a block; machines near spawn work again; a machine's permission check no longer uses the held item, is no longer refused at Mining Well tubes, and no longer pops pipe plugs; a refused placement of a two-block-tall block drops nothing; a changed `miningMultiplier` applies from game start; robots treat plants and fluids from mods that load after BuildCraft as soft, and those mods' fence gates, shulker boxes, dispensers and other vanilla-type blocks turn with the wrench; a vanilla cauldron takes and gives whole buckets only, so no sliver of fluid is made or lost; a mob's armour slots take one item at a time and a level gate on a composter's top no longer reads it as an empty 64-item slot; dropped items bob in crude oil and residue, and a ridden horse floats in crude oil without throwing its rider, as on NeoForge; and a transfer another mod leaves half-open no longer leaves BuildCraft's machines, pipes and tanks reacting late to every later change of their contents.
- Fixed machine screens showing wrong numbers on Fabric in the in-development build: engines (Stirling, combustion, FE engine, MJ dynamo) showed a tiny output, the wrong stored power and 0 °C heat; the Builder and Filler block counts broke past 32,767 blocks; and the Auto Workbench progress bar misread its power.
- Fixed the guide book's crafting and smelting panels being empty on a Fabric server: the server never sent the client its recipes.
- Fixed more Fabric visuals in the in-development build: inside oil, fuel or gas the view fogs to the fluid's own colour and closes in to a few blocks instead of showing water fog; hovering a used Blueprint, Template or Single Block Schematic shows its rotating 3D preview panel; fluids name their heat tier again (Oil (Cool), Oil (Hot), Oil (Searing) and so on) in tank tooltips, the iron engine screen, fluid shards and the guide, instead of every tier being plain "Oil"; and breaking or hitting a pipe shows particles in the pipe's own texture, and the Mining Well tube shows none, as on NeoForge.
- Fixed BuildCraft's own item and pipe looks on Fabric in the in-development build: facades, gates, lenses, filters and painted pipes draw as they do on NeoForge (in the inventory, in hand, dropped and travelling inside pipes) instead of a brick facade, a placeholder, nothing at all or an unpainted pipe; pipes and plugs are shaded face by face again, a painted pipe's glaze is see-through, and the glaze and lens and filter glass show their colour (on 1.21.1 too). The facade lists also leave out look-alike states (such as snowy podzol) as on NeoForge, and the Assembly Table again takes a look-alike block (a brick slab for the brick facade).
- Fixed pipes and engines (including the MJ dynamo) showing no crack while being mined on Minecraft 1.21.10 and later.
- Fixed dropped facades, gates, lenses and filters being invisible on Minecraft 1.21.10 and 1.21.11 (only their shadow showed), and sinking halfway into the ground on 26.1.2 and later.
- Fixed part-transparent GUI icons drawing their clear pixels solid dark on Minecraft 1.21.1 (the Filtered Buffer's "nothing here" marks, progress arrows and gauges, gate statement and engine status icons).
- Fixed stained glass, ice and slime facades on Minecraft 26.1.2 and later showing only their frame lines or drawing opaque; translucent facades are see-through again, in the world and in the inventory. Facades also break, hit-crack, and leave sprint and landing particles in the mimicked block's texture again, instead of the pipe's.
- Fixed the blueprint and snapshot 3D preview drawing dyed lenses and filters white and the clear lens untinted; they show their real colours, and plugs keep their face shading.
- Fixed brushing a pipe in multiplayer: the crack particles appear where the brushing player aims, instead of under another player's crosshair.
- Robot Goggles now say what the robot you are looking at is doing: a label floats over it (through walls), such as Charging, Out of power, Searching, Travelling, Loading, Breaking or Delivering, and a robot flying home to sleep, or asleep, says why - no tool, no work in zone, nothing to load, nowhere to unload, station busy or out of reach, blocked by protection, work that keeps failing, target full, no charging station, or no program. A search that found only blocks that refused the robot reads as blocked (or failing), and a block it found but could not reach as retrying, not as no work. A named robot shows its name with the status after it. Goggles also keep robots (and their outline) visible out to 64 blocks instead of 16. Also fixed a robot's name tag being drawn twice, which doubled its dark backdrop.
- Robots now break blocks the way a player does, so mods that react to a break see a robot's too: a tree-felling mod such as FallingTree brings a whole tree down when a Lumberjack robot cuts its bottom log (Lumberjacks now cut each trunk from the bottom up), and claim and logging mods are told. A Miner robot's ores now drop their experience orbs, and its tool wears by the item's own rule. With `minePlayerProtected` on, robots still break silently.
- Fixed `oilIsSticky` on multiplayer servers: each player's game used its own setting for boats and wading in oil; the server's setting now reaches every player, at login and whenever the config reloads.
- Fixed a tree-felling mod (such as FallingTree) felling a tree when a stripes pipe swings an axe at the air beside it: BuildCraft's protection checks now ask with an empty hand, not the item about to be used.
- Fixed lenses and filters in inventory slots drawing dimmer on NeoForge (Minecraft 1.21.11 and later) than on Fabric; they are as bright as in 1.12.2 again.
- Fixed a dispenser on Fabric setting a boat down on a gas (where it sinks); it ejects the boat item instead, as on NeoForge.
- Fixed facade items being drawn as a full block-sized panel when dropped, held or put in an item frame: they are block-item sized again, as in 1.12.2 (a quarter on the ground), and show their face in an item frame. The inventory icon is unchanged.
- Fixed pipe plug items (facades, pulsars, timers, light sensors, blockers and power adaptors) showing only their thin edge on a shelf (Minecraft 1.21.10 and later); they face out like their inventory icon. The same plug items in an item display with no display mode face the viewer too, instead of edge-on, and ride pipes turned the same way as gates, lenses and facades.
- Fixed red, blue and other dyed lenses and filters drawing plain grey in inventory slots on Fabric for Minecraft 1.21.10.
- Fixed dropped lenses and filters being about twice the size of a dropped block; they are block-item sized again, as in 1.12.2. Gates, lenses, filters and the other plugs now face out of an item frame instead of showing their edge, and a held gate is lit like the lens. Placed plugs are shaded by the side they actually face. Inventory icons and how they sit in the hand are unchanged.
- Fixed the game freezing for about a second and a half every time you join a world: BuildCraft's guide book now builds its pages the first time you open it, and that build is about three times faster than before.
