###### Changes since 2026.3.0:
- New: abandoned quarry pits are a rare surface find in the Overworld: a rotted quarry frame, full of gaps and cobwebs, over a square pit. Its quarry dug until it reached something worth finding, an ore, a mineshaft or a dungeon, and left it for the player, who can carry on the dig. Turn them off with the `generateQuarryPits` option.
