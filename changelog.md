###### Changes since 2026.4.0:

- **MinecraftForge support**, for those of you stuck with legacy modpacks. 1.21.10 is not supported by the Forge team, so I didn't bother with that version.
- When in creative mode, clicking a tank's gauge with a bucket on the cursor now works like using a bucket in the world instead of voiding the fluid.
- Minecraft 1.21.10 and later (NeoForge): right-clicking a tank with a filled bucket no longer duplicates the bucket (you used to get the empty bucket and keep the full one), and holding a stack of buckets now fills or empties just one of them. Thank you for reporting, [user_pf5hyk9zed69zy64](https://www.curseforge.com/members/user_pf5hyk9zed69zy64)!

Other than that, there are some minor bug fixes.

- The description of the "Mine Player Protected Blocks" setting is more descriptive
- Speaking of which, with "Mine Player Protected Blocks" off, a Builder, Quarry, Mining Well or Stripes pipe that a claim mod stops from breaking a block now skips it and carries on, instead of retrying it forever or stalling. The Builder's status now says the block was refused.
- Robots now actually report and acknowledge when an action was rejected due to being within a claim instead of retrying forever.
- Robots no longer fly back and forth forever when their target sits inside a block they can't enter, such as a Picker going after an item lying on a pipe. A Picker or a fighting robot now does its job from beside it, and other robots give up on it. A robot also no longer flies into a block that turned up on the first step of a route it had already worked out; it looks for another way round instead.
- Improved and fixed rendering of pipes that are being placed or in blueprints.
- With ModernFix's dynamic resources option on, gates, facades and lenses show their proper item icons again, and pipe items get their colours back.
- With Sodium installed (NeoForge), gate, lens, facade and painted pipe item icons are no longer drawn too dark.
- Pipe wires now join up straight away when a pipe next to them is replaced, instead of leaving gaps or loose ends until the world is reloaded; a removed pipe also no longer leaves an invisible wire stub sticking out of its neighbour that you could bump into.
- Pipe wire signals now follow the wires you can see. Putting a plug or solid facade between two wired pipes, repainting a pipe, or pipes connecting or disconnecting cuts or joins the signal straight away, and breaking a wired pipe (or losing one to an explosion or a command) no longer leaves the wires next to it dead until you re-place a wire or reload the world. Editing a pipe with `/data` is picked up as well. A Power Adaptor, or a Docking Station with a robot on it, now cuts wires the way a plug or a solid facade does: a wire used to join across one from the kinesis pipe's side only, which could leave that wire without a signal. A wire that was cut off from its network while its area was unloaded now rejoins as soon as the area loads, instead of staying dark until you get close enough for it to be simulated.
- Worlds with large pipe wire networks load faster, and a gate changing its wire signal no longer makes the server load far-away chunks that hold other wired gates. Placing a block beside a wired pipe, or a wired area loading in, also no longer makes the server send every pipe wire network to nearby players again.
- A blueprint built rotated now turns everything in it. Inside pipes: pipe wires, the direction of wooden, iron, emerald, emzuli, daizuli and stripes pipes, a diamond pipe's filters, the sides a gate's triggers and actions read, and the direction of the block a facade shows (a log or glazed terracotta facade keeps its direction relative to the build). Machines: lasers, chutes, distillers, heat exchangers, mining wells and zone planners now face the rotated way, a flood gate's closed sides and an engine's output side turn too, a Filler keeps its pattern pointing the right way, and a Builder inside the blueprint keeps building its own blueprint the right way round. All of these used to stay the way they were captured, so a setup only worked when it was built facing the way it was copied. The same blocks now also turn when a structure block or another mod rotates them.
- A machine built from a blueprint no longer carries on with the working area of the machine it was copied from: a copied Filler used to keep filling the original's box, a copied Builder followed the original's path, a Quarry copied beside the original's frame mined the original's pit, and an Architect Table, Mining Well or Laser started from the original's area or target. A copy now starts without the original's area. A Quarry built from a blueprint lays out its area as one you place does: the volume box or markers behind it if there are any, otherwise its default area. Once powered it digs that area like any Quarry, so leave the space behind it free in the blueprint.
- A machine or pipe built from a blueprint now belongs to whoever built it (the owner of the Builder, or the player who placed it from a single schematic; nobody, when nobody owns the Builder), not to whoever made the blueprint, so a copy of someone's blueprint no longer digs, places or asks claim mods as that player.
- A Quarry no longer takes markers that it is not allowed to break (in someone else's claim, say): it leaves them standing and uses its default area instead, whether you place it or a Builder builds it.
- The "Logic transportation" advancement is no longer granted for a wire placed beside one it does not connect to (across a plug or a solid facade, or between two pipes that do not connect).
- A Quarry placed against a marker area that was resized with a wrench now uses the resized area, even when the drag left the bottom of the box below the Quarry or its top above it. Markers and boxes now share one minimum of 3x3; anything narrower gets the default area instead.
