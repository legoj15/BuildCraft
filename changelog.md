###### Changes since 2026.3.0:

* The Heat Exchanger has a new look: an iron shell-and-tube vessel with bolted joints between segments, capped ends, and blue (cold) and red (hot) nozzles where fluids go in and out. Slots banded in dark green on all four long sides show the fluids flowing, top and bottom included. Its recipe takes a copper ingot in the centre in place of the middle glass. Extending a working Heat Exchanger past five blocks now shuts it down cleanly instead of leaving a stray end cap mid-row that kept running, and a Heat Exchanger that stops working because its row grew too long or was cut too short spills the fluid it held rather than losing it.
* Lava now sets placed oils and fuels alight, like fire already does. Lava never lights crude oil itself, so oil lakes beside lava pools and the oil slicks on the Nether's lava sea aren't set ablaze by the lava alone; residue still never burns.
* **Robot Goggles** can now be crafted from iron ingots, glass panes and a redstone chipset, and worn in the head slot. They outline every robot through walls, red when its battery is empty to green when it is full, and outline the cells a stalled Builder is skipping.
* Abandoned quarry pits are a rare surface find in the Overworld: a rotted quarry frame, full of gaps and cobwebs, over a square pit. Its quarry dug until it reached something worth finding, an ore, a mineshaft or a dungeon, and left it for the player, who can carry on the dig. Turn them off with the `generateQuarryPits` option.
* Fixed a rare crash when chunks holding Timers, Light Sensors, Pulsars, Lenses or Filters loaded. Lenses and Filters also pick up resource-pack texture changes after a reload now. Resource packs can now reshape Timers, Light Sensors and Pulsars (`models/plugs/timer.json`, `light_sensor.json`, `pulsar_static.json`) like the Blocker and Power Adapter.
* Gates now see the cauldrons and composters their pipe connects to: the fluid and inventory triggers (full, empty, contains, below 25/50/75%) work on them, including the fluid-filtered ones.
* New option, "Enable Detonation of Contained Fluids" (off by default): gaseous fuel in a tank or fluid pipe can now explode when fire, lava or a lit campfire is beside it, or when a blast breaks the container. "Per block" makes any container holding at least a bucket go off like a block of gas; "by volume" scales the blast with how much it holds, from a faint pop for a nearly empty pipe to well beyond TNT for a full tank. It does nothing while oils and fuels can't burn or gas can't explode.
* Pipes, gates, lenses, Pulsars, Docking Stations, dyed pipe borders and gate icons now pick up texture changes after a resource-pack change, the High Contrast toggle or F3+T, instead of sometimes keeping the old textures until a restart.
* Redrew the Filler's pattern and option icons in one consistent style, so each shape is readable at a glance; the axis options mark their axis in the red, green and blue of 3D editors, and the side options colour their side the way gate tooltips colour side names (except Bottom, black rather than the tooltips' dark grey), with its letter (N, S, E, W, T, B). Hollow, Filled and Surrounded keep the original icons' design: one ring, with a checker fill showing where blocks go. The gate's pipe-direction actions share the new side icons.
* The Filler has a **Flatten** pattern again: it clears the marked box and lays a floor along its bottom layer. The Octagon pattern now builds an octagon (it placed nothing before), the Hexagon's Filled option now fills it, and 2D shapes turned a quarter or three-quarter turn in a non-square box fill properly (Filled used to do nothing and Surrounded filled the whole box). An option slot greyed out for the chosen pattern, trigger or action no longer keeps a value from the one chosen before it: Square and Octagon no longer pick up a hidden turn, a gate's Empty and Full inventory and tank triggers no longer quietly keep the item or fluid filter of a Contains trigger they replaced, and greyed-out slots no longer open a menu or take a dropped value. A gate action that sets a Filler pattern now passes on its first option too (a Circle on the X axis set by a gate used to build on Y).
