###### Changes since 2026.2.0:

- Added Minecraft 26.3 support. Minimum required NeoForge version is 26.3.0.38.
- Robots are now interpolated like everything else (i.e. they move smoothly).
- Robots and blocks that can modify the world now call the exact same functions players do, which allows land claiming/property protection server plugins/mods to stay enforced.
- Stripes pipes and robots now use fluid containers and thrown items on the block in front (1.7.10 feature).
- Stripes pipes and robots also now keep the fluid container after using up contents.
- Stripes pipes now extend their line when fed item pipes, and break minecarts in front of them (1.7.10 feature).
- Builders and Fillers no longer get stuck next to fluids they can't remove, and builds with fluid blocks where there is empty space now complete successfully. In keep-fluid (No-Replace) mode the Builder now waits for nearby moving water before placing torches, snow and other fragile blocks, and places blocks the blueprint wants waterlogged into water of their own kind instead of skipping them. A block that fails to place, from the Builder or a single schematic, no longer deletes the water or lava source, waterlogged block, grass, seagrass, snow or flowing water it was placed into. The Builder now breaks a waterlogged block in Replace mode, or a block placed in a spot it was about to fill, like any other wrong block (keeping its drop and contents) instead of building over it.
- Quarries now repair a frame gap once whatever blocked it is gone (such as a protected area being unclaimed).
- Mining Wells and Pumps no longer delete waterlogged blocks.
- Idle Pumps over huge pools and the Zone Planner map aren't performance hogs any more, and Pumps and robots no longer load or generate chunks while searching, pathing or draining (a pool next to an unloaded chunk is pumped once it loads).
- Machines keep or drop their contents correctly when blown up or replaced by commands.
- Stacks above 99 (from mods that raise stack limits) are no longer lost when saved in pipes, Assembly Tables, Builders, item lists or robot requests.
- Fluid pipes no longer create free fluid when another mod test-fills them and then cancels the transfer.
- {1.21.1} /setblock, /fill and /clone no longer spill contents when breaking machines (matches the newer MC versions).
- Fixed the Distiller so that it now drops the items in its container slots.
- The Electronic Library no longer rejects blueprints holding pistons or blocks with replaceable variants. Older saved blueprints will be repaired automatically.
- Fixed an edge case in the Guide Book where text would render over page numbers, and links that wrap onto the next page are now clickable on both pages.
- The guide book's Sort By System button has its own icon instead of the old Sort By Mod one.
- {1.21.10/1.21.11/26.1/26.1.1/26.1.2/26.2} Fixed Fragile Fluid Shards showing the wrong color for colored fluids (lava shards looked blue) on Minecraft 1.21.10 and newer.
- The Wooden Diamond pipe's filter modes are now called Allowlist and Blocklist since that is easier to translate.
- In the off chance where you'd have more than 24 robot boards, the programming table can now advance to the next page.
- Blueprints now charge for the contents of everything they rebuild (including copper chests, shelves, crafters, lecterns, jukeboxes, decorated pots, and armor stand gear on 1.21.1) and build empty whatever they can't charge for, leaving off pipe attachments they can't price; decorated pots keep their sherds and charge for them, and entities that a mod's rules whitelist without pricing are no longer captured or built for free; rebuilt machines start with no stored power, spawners keep their mob, trial spawners and vaults are rebuilt fresh, and the Builder's ignore-contents setting now also covers armor stands, item frames and minecarts. Bad blueprint data (including Electronic Library uploads) can no longer crash the Builder, smuggle in loot tables, or place blocks the Architect would never scan.
- Blueprints charge waterlogged blocks, kelp and seagrass as themselves instead of as water, keep a wet pipe's pipe and attachments, and no longer copy Mining Well and Pump tubes; older blueprints repair their waterlogged blocks as they load but leave out wet pipes, whose pipe was never saved.
- Blueprints stored on a server can now only be downloaded by a player holding an item that references them; a preview the server refused (such as one shown in a storage terminal) tries again instead of staying blank.
- Freshly scanned blueprints containing stairs are no longer refused by the Electronic Library or lost track of after a restart.
- The Architect Table refuses to scan an area too large for its blueprint to load again after a restart, telling you why, instead of saving a blueprint that later goes missing.
- Written Zone Planner maps keep their zone with the world instead of inside the item, so copies and stacks no longer bloat item data; existing maps convert automatically, and maps saved in a blueprint keep their zone when built in another world (a zone map in a blueprint costs a blank map); a crash can no longer leave two maps naming the same zone (unless the world's zone-map file could not be written).
- The Electronic Library's upload size setting now tops out at 128 MB, the most a server can read back (higher settings are lowered to 128), and checking a large upload no longer takes several times its size in server memory.
- A malicious or hijacked server can no longer freeze or crash clients through oversized values in BuildCraft's network packets, and a dimension with tens of thousands of markers no longer breaks marker syncing for players joining it (this changes the marker packet, so older clients and servers are refused at login instead of misreading it).
- Robots now remember who deployed them: anyone can still deploy a robot on a free docking station, but it only breaks, places, uses tools and attacks (bomber TNT and its whole blast included) where both its deployer and the station's owner are allowed to, and protection mods and logs see it as the player who deployed it, so its kills drop experience and player-only loot, and its weapon's Looting now applies. Robots homed on a station from before this update stay idle until that station has loaded once.
- Robot pathfinding and pipe wires are no longer performance hogs, pipe wires now show their state to players who walk into range or rejoin (without being re-sent on every loaded chunk, and without leftovers after changing dimension), and the Flood Gate now fills from a bounded search around itself instead of scanning an entire lake.
- The Filler Planner menu now closes when its box is removed or you walk away, and creative middle-click clones of pipes, filtered buffers and the Zone Planner no longer carry the block's saved data.
