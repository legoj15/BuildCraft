###### Changes since 2026.3.2:

- **Fabric support for Minecraft 26.3 (in development).** BuildCraft now also builds for Fabric (with Fabric API and Forge Config API Port); the NeoForge versions are unchanged. Machines respect the Fabric claim mods that answer through the bundled Common Protection API, placements included (a claim mod that guards placements only through its own mixins is not asked about a placement). Known Fabric differences so far: no Curios/Accessories slot for the Robot Goggles, fluids move in whole millibuckets (a transfer of less than 1 mB moves nothing), blueprint and schematic tooltip previews are not drawn, the Mining Well tube keeps vanilla's block particles, and the fog in oil is vanilla's.
- Fixed mobs and players being stuck in place in crude oil and residue on Minecraft 26.1.2 and later: they now wade through slowly (residue the slowest, and slower again with `oilIsSticky`), sink gently, and can climb out at the edge.
- Fixed a blank facade (one with no block, e.g. from `/give`) placing onto a pipe as an invisible cover; it is now refused, and no placement outline is shown for it. Likewise, a lens no longer shows a placement outline on a pipe that is not an item pipe, nor a power adaptor or pulsar on a pipe engines cannot power, since none of them can be placed there.
- Fixed a living entity's hands and armour reading as one unnamed slot to BuildCraft's machines on Fabric, instead of the six indexed slots NeoForge's entity item capability hands out.
- The Fabric build now asks only for the oldest Fabric Loader (0.19.4) and Fabric API (0.160.6) it has been tested on, instead of the newest builds at the time it was made.
- Fixed every BuildCraft chunk loader silently stopping after switching worlds on Fabric: the chunk tickets' owner bookkeeping was never cleared when a world unloaded, so the previous world's records told the new one its chunks were already ticketed.
- Fixed more Fabric behaviour in the in-development build: claim mods are asked before a wrench turns a block; machines near spawn work again; a machine's permission check no longer uses the held item, is no longer refused at Mining Well tubes, and no longer pops pipe plugs; a refused placement of a two-block-tall block drops nothing; a changed `miningMultiplier` applies from game start; robots treat plants and fluids from mods that load after BuildCraft as soft, and those mods' fence gates, shulker boxes, dispensers and other vanilla-type blocks turn with the wrench; a vanilla cauldron takes and gives whole buckets only, so no sliver of fluid is made or lost; a mob's armour slots take one item at a time and a level gate on a composter's top no longer reads it as an empty 64-item slot; dropped items bob in crude oil and residue and boats sink in oil and fuel, as on NeoForge; and a transfer another mod leaves half-open no longer leaves BuildCraft's machines, pipes and tanks reacting late to every later change of their contents.
- Fixed the guide book's crafting and smelting panels being empty on a Fabric server: the server never sent the client its recipes.
