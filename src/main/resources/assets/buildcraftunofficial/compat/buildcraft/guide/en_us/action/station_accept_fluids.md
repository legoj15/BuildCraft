<no_lore>
Accept Fluids is a gate action offered on any pipe that carries a Docking Station. While it is active, robots may empty their tanks into that station's pipe.
</no_lore>

<chapter name="Information"/>
The station's pipe must be a fluid pipe. Tank robots and Pump robots unload here; a fluid container in the parameter slot limits which fluid is taken. The gate must be firing for the action to count; a trigger that is always true (Redstone Signal Off with no redstone nearby) is the usual way to keep it on.
