Force Robot is a gate action offered on any pipe that carries a Docking Station. Only robots matching its parameter may use that station.
</no_lore>

<chapter name="Information"/>
Click the parameter slot with a programmed robot, or with an empty hand to cycle through the boards, and the station is reserved for robots of that kind; everyone else is turned away. Handy for keeping the Knight's sword chest away from the Butcher, or a charging dock free for the robot that matters. It does not hand over a station another robot lives at: a robot living there that does not match leaves, but the station stays its home and no one else may use it. To free the station, pick up the robot that lives there, then place the other robot on it. With the slot empty every robot is turned away. Needs a Quartz or Diamond gate for the parameter slot.
