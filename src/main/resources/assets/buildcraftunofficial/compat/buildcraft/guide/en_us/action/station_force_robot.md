Force Robot is a gate action offered on any pipe that carries a Docking Station. Only robots matching its parameter may use that station.
</no_lore>

<chapter name="Information"/>
Put a robot or a board in the parameter slot and the station is reserved for robots of that kind; everyone else is turned away. Handy for keeping the Knight's sword chest away from the Butcher, or a charging dock free for the robot that matters. Needs a Quartz or Diamond gate for the parameter slot.
