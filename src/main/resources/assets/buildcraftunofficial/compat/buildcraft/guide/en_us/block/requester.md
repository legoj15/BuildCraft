<lore>
An order form in block form. Write down what you want in the top half, and Delivery robots keep the bottom half stocked.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Requester asks Delivery robots for items. It holds twenty requests and twenty matching delivery slots; robots fill the delivery slots from any station that provides the requested items.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Using it"/>
Place the Requester next to a pipe that carries a <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:item/robot_station"/>; the station finds it on any side. Click an item into one of the twenty request slots in the upper half, holding as many as you want delivered, and that becomes a standing order. A Delivery robot takes the order, loads the item at a station whose gate has Provide Items active, flies back and puts it into the delivery slot below the request. Deliveries can be pulled out by hand or by any pipe; a slot accepts only the item requested above it, so nothing else can be hoppered in.
Each order is served by one robot at a time, and a robot that cannot find the item gives up on it until its next search.
