<chapter name="Behaviour"/>
The robot equips a sword from a Provide Items station inside its zone and attacks animals it finds there, leaving the drops on the ground for a Picker. It does not spare breeding pairs, so fence off the stock you want to keep. Costs 3,200 MJ to program.
