<lore>
Not the one who sows or reaps: the one who tills.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Farmer board makes a robot till dirt and grass into farmland inside its work zone, using a hoe fetched from a station.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Behaviour"/>
The robot equips a hoe from a Provide Items station inside its zone and hoes every dirt or grass block it finds there. It refuses a worn-out hoe and fetches a fresh one. Costs 3,200 MJ to program.
