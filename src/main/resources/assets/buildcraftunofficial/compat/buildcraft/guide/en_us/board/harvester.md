<lore>
It only touches what is ripe, and never tramples the field.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Harvester board makes a robot harvest fully grown crops inside its work zone. It needs no tool.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Behaviour"/>
The robot flies to each mature crop in its zone and harvests it, leaving the drops on the ground and the farmland intact. Immature crops are left alone. Costs 3,200 MJ to program.
<hint>
Harvester, Planter and Picker on the same field make a complete farm; a Farmer keeps the soil tilled.
</hint>
