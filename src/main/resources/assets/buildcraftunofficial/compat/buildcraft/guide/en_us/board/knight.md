A sword, a zone to patrol, and no fear whatsoever.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Knight board makes a robot attack hostile mobs inside its work zone with a sword fetched from a station.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Behaviour"/>
The robot equips a sword from a Provide Items station inside its zone, then hunts monsters (and angry wolves) up to 250 blocks away within the zone, flying to each and striking until it dies. A worn-out sword is swapped for a fresh one. Mob drops are left on the ground. Costs 12,800 MJ to program.
