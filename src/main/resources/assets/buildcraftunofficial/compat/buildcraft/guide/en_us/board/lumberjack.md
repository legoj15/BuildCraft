<lore>
Give it an axe and a forest and come back later for a clearing.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Lumberjack board makes a robot fell logs inside its work zone using an axe fetched from a station.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Behaviour"/>
The robot first equips an axe from a station inside its zone whose gate has Provide Items active, then breaks every log it can reach in the zone. The logs drop on the ground; it does not collect them. When the axe breaks it fetches another, and with no axe or no logs it sleeps. Costs 3,200 MJ to program.
<hint>
A Picker robot on the same zone keeps up with the drops; a Planter with saplings makes the forest grow back.
</hint>
