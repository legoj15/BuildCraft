<lore>
A prospector that never sleeps in the mine, only on its perch.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Miner board makes a robot mine ore blocks inside its work zone with a pickaxe fetched from a station.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Behaviour"/>
The robot equips a pickaxe from a Provide Items station inside its zone and breaks ores it finds there. It only mines what the pickaxe it holds could mine: a stone pick leaves iron and better alone, an iron pick leaves diamonds and obsidian. The drops fall where the ore was. Costs 3,200 MJ to program.
<hint>
The zone is a column, so a zone painted over a cave system mines every ore from bedrock to surface. Add a Picker, or you will be collecting by hand.
</hint>
