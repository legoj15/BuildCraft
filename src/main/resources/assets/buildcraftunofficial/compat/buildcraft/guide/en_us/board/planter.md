Hand it a sack of seeds and it finds the empty soil for you.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Planter board makes a robot plant seeds and saplings on suitable ground inside its work zone.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Behaviour"/>
The robot fetches seeds, saplings or other plantable items from a Provide Items station inside its zone, then looks for a spot where that plant can grow, flies there and plants it. It will not plant where the crop could not survive, so a dark field stays empty until it is lit. Costs 3,200 MJ to program.
