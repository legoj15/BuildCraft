<lore>
A pump that comes to the puddle instead of waiting for the puddle to come to it.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Pump board makes a robot drain fluid source blocks inside its work zone into its own tank and unload them at a station.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Behaviour"/>
The robot finds a fluid source block in its zone, flies to it and pumps it into its 4,000 mB tank, then carries the fluid to a station whose gate has Accept Fluids active. That station must stand inside the work zone, or inside a Load/Unload in Area zone if the home gate sets one; a home station outside the zone is only used when the robot comes back to sleep, so it unloads about one bucket a minute. It needs no tool. Costs 3,200 MJ to program.
<hint>
Good for mopping up an oil spring or a flooded quarry pit when a fixed <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:block/pump"/> is in the wrong place.
</hint>
