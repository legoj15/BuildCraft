<lore>
Dirt, sand, gravel: if a shovel digs it, this robot removes it.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Shovelman board makes a robot dig shovel blocks inside its work zone using a shovel fetched from a station.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Behaviour"/>
The robot equips a shovel from a Provide Items station inside its zone and digs out the dirt, sand, gravel and similar blocks in it, leaving the drops where they fall. Costs 3,200 MJ to program.
<hint>
Paint the zone carefully. It will happily dig the ground out from under your own buildings.
</hint>
