Uhh... Stripes! A Stripes pipe that got up and flew away.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Stripes board makes a robot use whatever item it is given on empty spots inside its work zone, exactly as a Stripes pipe would use the item.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Behaviour"/>
The robot takes any item from a Provide Items station inside its zone, finds a free block of air in the zone and uses the item there the way a <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:pipe/stripes_item"/> does: blocks get placed, seeds planted, buckets emptied, and so on. It repeats until the station runs dry. Costs 12,800 MJ to program.
