<lore>
The Carrier's wetter cousin: a flying bucket with a four-bucket belly.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Tank board makes a robot move fluids from stations that provide them to stations that accept them. The robot carries 4,000 mB.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Behaviour"/>
Empty, the robot fills up at a station whose gate has Provide Fluids active, through that station's Wooden fluid pipe; full, it empties into a station with Accept Fluids active. The gate's Filter action limits which fluid it takes. Costs 800 MJ to program.
