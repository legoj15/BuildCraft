The Volume Box is only available in creative mode or with cheats. It is manipulable with a Volume Wrench.
<link to="buildcraftunofficial:item/volume_wrench"/>
Click the corner of it you want to move with the Volume Wrench and move where you are pointing and the box size will move with you.
Click with the wrench again where you want to stop it moving and it will stop. You can shift click it with a Volume Wrench or Marker Wrench to remove the Volume Marker completely
