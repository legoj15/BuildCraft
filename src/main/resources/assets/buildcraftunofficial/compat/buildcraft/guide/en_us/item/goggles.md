<lore>
It's easy to get lost in your work. Literally. Put these on to at least keep track of where your robots are and what your machines are doing.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Robot Goggles are a head-slot item that outlines robots through solid blocks, and shows the cells a stalled Builder is skipping.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Information"/>
Equip the item to use it. It goes in the helmet armor slot, Curios head slot, or Accessories face slot if those mods are installed. They cannot be enchanted, provide no armor, and cannot be damaged.
While they are on, every <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:item/robot"/> in range is outlined through blocks. The outline shows its charge: red when the battery is empty, green when it is full, mixing the colors when inbetween. A robot low enough to go looking for a charging station is already mostly red. The outlines are only visible to you.
They also outline the cells a stalled <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:block/builder"/> is skipping, the same outlines a <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:item/marker_wrench"/> or a <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:item/volume_wrench"/> in your hand shows. Red marks a cell you have to fix yourself, such as a block that cannot stand where the blueprint puts it or one that cannot be broken. Yellow marks a cell waiting on supplies, and blue one held back by fluid.
