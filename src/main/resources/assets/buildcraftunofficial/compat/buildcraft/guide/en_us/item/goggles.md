<lore>
Robots fly off to work and vanish behind walls, trees and hillsides, and you only learn a battery has run flat when one drops out of the sky. A redstone chipset set in the bridge of a pair of goggles shows you where every robot is and how much power it has left.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Robot Goggles are a head-slot item that outlines robots through solid blocks, coloured by their charge, and shows the cells a stalled Builder is skipping.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Information"/>
Wear them in the head slot, or right-click with them to put them on. They give no armour, never wear out and cannot be enchanted.
While they are on, every <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:item/robot"/> in range is outlined through walls, trees and hillsides. The outline shows its charge: red when the battery is empty, green when it is full, and a dimmer blend of the two in between. A robot low enough to go looking for a charging station is already mostly red. Only you see the outlines; they are not part of the world.
They also outline the cells a stalled <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:block/builder"/> is skipping, the same outlines a <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:item/marker_wrench"/> or a <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:item/volume_wrench"/> in your hand shows. Red marks a cell you have to fix yourself, such as a block that cannot stand where the blueprint puts it or one that cannot be broken. Yellow marks a cell waiting on supplies, and blue one held back by fluid.
