<lore>
When working on creating paths or volumes, it seriously helps to be able to see the connections live.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Marker Wrench is an upgraded wrench that lets you see and gauge marker connections before they are applied.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Information"/>
The Marker Wrench does everything the <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:item/wrench"/> does, with the added ability to see marker connections before they are applied, and to keep track of the distances between them or you for easier planning. Linking markers and handling boxes costs no durability.
To visualize connections and see current connection lengths, hold the tool in your hand. To measure distance between yourself and a marker, right-click on it and start walking in a straight line. A distance readout will appear beneath the crosshair. This is the distance from you to the marker, not the total length. The distance readout will persist even after putting the tool away. To remove it, either finish linking markers together, or right-click in the air.
To delete a volume you have created, either break a <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:block/marker_volume"/>, break the connected machine, or crouch and right-click on it if the markers are already gone without a paired machine.
