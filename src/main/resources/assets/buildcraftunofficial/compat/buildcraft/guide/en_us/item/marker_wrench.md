<lore>
Finer control over the areas that your machines work is vital in any large scale engineering project and you need a tool to control that.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Marker Wrench is a wrench that also links markers together and looks after the boxes machines work in.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Information"/>
The Marker Wrench does everything the <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:item/wrench"/> does, so it still turns blocks and machines with a click, and it wears down and mends just like it: one use per turn, hit or mined block, and iron ingots on an anvil. Linking markers and handling boxes costs it nothing.
Hold it and the links you can make between markers appear as thin lasers. Aim at one and click to link those two markers. A click that lands on a laser links it instead of turning the block behind it.
Crouch-click a floating box that no machine has taken to throw it away. To resize an area you have marked out, or drag the corners of any other area, you need the <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:item/volume_wrench"/>.
