<lore>
Finer control over the areas that your machines work is vital in any large scale engineering project and you need a tool to control that.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Marker Wrench is a wrench that also links markers together and looks after the boxes machines work in.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Information"/>
The Marker Wrench does everything the <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:item/wrench"/> does, so it still turns blocks and machines with a click.
Hold it and the links you can make between markers appear as thin lasers. Aim at one and click to link those two markers. A click that lands on a laser links it instead of turning the block behind it.
It also resizes an area you have marked out. Right-click one of the corner markers of a connected volume and move where you are pointing: the area lifts off its markers into a floating box and the corner follows. Click again to keep the new size and the markers drop as items. Crouch and click to cancel (so does putting the wrench away, or clicking without having changed the size) and the markers stay as they were. A Quarry, Filler or Architect Table placed against a floating box takes it the way it takes markers: the Quarry uses it up, while a Filler or Architect Table keeps it as its own area. Crouch-click a floating box that no machine has taken to throw it away.
The Marker Wrench can only drag the corner of an area it is lifting off markers. To drag the corners of any other area, you need the <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:item/volume_wrench"/>.
