<lore>
Finer control over the areas that your machines work is vital in any large scale engineering project and you need a tool to control that.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Marker Wrench is a wrench that also links markers together and looks after the boxes machines work in.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Information"/>
The Marker Wrench does everything the <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:item/wrench"/> does, so it still turns blocks and machines with a click.
Hold it and the links you can make between markers appear as thin lasers. Aim at one and click to link those two markers. A click that lands on a laser links it instead of turning the block behind it.
You can also click an addon on a machine's working area to use it, and crouch-click an area no machine is using to remove it. While an area is being resized, click again to keep the new size, or crouch and click to put it back.
To drag the corners of an area yourself, you need the <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:item/volume_wrench"/>.
