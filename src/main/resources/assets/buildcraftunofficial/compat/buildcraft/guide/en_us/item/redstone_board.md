<lore>
Eight sheets of paper around a lump of redstone. It does not look like much, but once the Programming Table has burned a job into it, this is the whole personality of a robot.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Redstone Board carries a robot's program. A blank board is crafted by hand; the Programming Table writes a job onto it, and the Integration Table fits the written board into a Robot.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Information"/>
A board stays blank until it goes through the <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:block/programming_table"/>, where you pick the job from every board the game knows and pay its cost in laser power. The written board's name and icon change to match the job, and its tooltip says what the job does. Blank boards stack to 16, written ones to 1.
Boards cost 800 MJ for the simple hauling jobs (Picker, Carrier, Tank), 3,200 MJ for the jobs that work a zone (Lumberjack, Miner, Farmer, Planter, Harvester, Leaf Cutter, Shovelman, Pump, Butcher), 12,800 MJ for the Knight, Delivery, Bomber and Stripes boards, and 51,200 MJ for the Builder board. Each board has its own page under Boards.
