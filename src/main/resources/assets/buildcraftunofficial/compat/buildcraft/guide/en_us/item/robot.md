<lore>
Five iron, a redstone crystal and two diamond chipsets: a body with nothing in its head. Until a board goes in it is just an expensive paperweight.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Robot is the body of an autonomous flying worker. On its own it does nothing; it must be combined with a programmed Redstone Board in the Integration Table before it can be placed.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Information"/>
A blank robot cannot be placed: right-clicking a station with one shows "Not programmed" and keeps the item. Program it in the <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:block/integration_table"/> with a board from the <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:block/programming_table"/>, then right-click a <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:item/robot_station"/> on a pipe to put it to work. Blank robots stack to 16; programmed ones carry their board, charge and name, so they stack to 1.
The tooltip shows the board inside and the charge. A robot holds 10,000 MJ and goes looking for a charging station below 2,000 MJ; the Integration Table tops a drained robot up to that floor when it programs it.
<hint>
Keep a charging station on a kinesis pipe near every work zone: a robot that has to fly a long way to charge spends most of its battery commuting.
</hint>
