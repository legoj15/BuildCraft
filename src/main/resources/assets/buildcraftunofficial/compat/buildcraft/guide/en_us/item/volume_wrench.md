<lore>
Marking out an area is only half the job. The other half is changing it once the machine is running.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Volume Wrench is a Marker Wrench that can also resize the working area of a machine.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Information"/>
The Volume Wrench does everything the <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:item/marker_wrench"/> does, and it can also drag the corners of a machine's working area.
Click the corner you want to move, then move where you are pointing and the area follows. Click again to keep the new size, or crouch and click to put it back the way it was.
Edits are capped at the range markers can reach. Changing an area restarts the machine's job.
