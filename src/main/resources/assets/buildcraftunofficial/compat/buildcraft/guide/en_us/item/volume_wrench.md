<no_lore>
The Volume Wrench is a Marker Wrench that can also resize a marked-out area or the working area of a machine.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Information"/>
The Volume Wrench does everything the <link inline="buildcraftunofficial:item/marker_wrench"/> does, and it can also drag the corners of any area: the working area of a Filler or Architect Table that took over markers, or a floating box left behind by resizing markers.
It also resizes an area you have marked out. Right-click one of the corner markers of a connected volume and move where you are pointing: the area lifts off its markers into a floating box and the corner follows. Click again to keep the new size and the markers drop as items. Crouch and click to cancel (so does clicking without having changed the size) and the markers stay as they were. A Quarry, Filler or Architect Table placed against a floating box takes it the way it takes markers: the Quarry uses it up, while a Filler or Architect Table keeps it as its own area. Crouch-click a floating box that no machine has taken to throw it away.
Click the corner you want to move, then move where you are pointing and the area follows. Click again to keep the new size, or crouch and click to put it back the way it was. Put the wrench away mid-drag and the drag is cancelled.
It is made of diamond, so it lasts about six times as long as the other wrenches and is mended with diamonds, but only turning blocks, hitting and mining wear it: handling boxes costs nothing.
Edits are capped at the range markers can reach. Changing a machine's area restarts the machine's job, and the area goes away when the machine is broken.
