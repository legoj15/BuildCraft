# Bomber Board

Some robots carry an axe. This one carries TNT, and it is not careful with it.

## Behaviour

The robot loads TNT from a station whose gate has Provide Items active, picks a random spot of open ground in its zone, flies twenty blocks above it and drops a primed charge, then goes back for more. The explosions are ordinary TNT explosions and destroy whatever is below. Costs 12,800 MJ to program.
