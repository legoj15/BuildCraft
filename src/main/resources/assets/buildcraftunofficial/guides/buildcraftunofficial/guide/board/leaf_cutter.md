# Leaf Cutter Board

A pair of shears with wings. Trees get a haircut; the trunks are someone else's job.

## Behaviour

The robot equips shears from a Provide Items station inside its zone and cuts every leaf block it finds there. The drops fall to the ground. Costs 3,200 MJ to program.
