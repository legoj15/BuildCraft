# Miner Board

A prospector that never sleeps in the mine, only on its perch.

## Behaviour

The robot equips a pickaxe from a Provide Items station inside its zone and breaks ores it finds there. It only mines ores the pickaxe it holds could mine: a wooden pick takes only coal and nether gold, a stone pick adds iron, copper and lapis, and an iron pick adds gold, redstone, diamond and emerald. Only ore blocks count; stone, obsidian and other blocks are never touched. The drops fall where the ore was. Costs 3,200 MJ to program.
