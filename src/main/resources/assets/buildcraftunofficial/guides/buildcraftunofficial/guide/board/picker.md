# Picker Board

The tidy-up robot. Anything lying on the ground inside its zone, it fetches and brings home.

## Behaviour

The robot looks for dropped items up to 250 blocks away inside its Work in Area zone, flies to each and picks it up until its four cargo slots are full or nothing is left, then unloads at the nearest station whose gate has Accept Items active. With nothing to collect it sleeps at home and looks again a minute later. Needs no tool. Costs 800 MJ to program.
