# Stripes Board

Uhh... Stripes! A Stripes pipe that got up and flew away.

## Behaviour

The robot takes any item from a Provide Items station inside its zone, finds a free block of air in the zone and uses the item there the way a [Stripes Transport Pipe](../pipe/stripes_item.md) does: blocks get placed, seeds planted, buckets emptied, and so on. It repeats until the station runs dry. Costs 12,800 MJ to program.
