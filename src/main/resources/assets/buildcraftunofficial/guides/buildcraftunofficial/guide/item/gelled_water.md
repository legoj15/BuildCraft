# Gelled Water

<ItemImage id="buildcraftunofficial:gelled_water" scale="2" float="right" />

You need some water gone? It's easier to handle when it's a solid.

## Information

Obtained by using a **Water Gellifier** on a water source, Gelled Water is obtained by waiting for the water to reach its final solid form. One gellifier will affect multiple water sources, so bulk clearing/creation of gelled water is possible. Water will not solidify into its final form if a water source is touching it, so the borders of a chunk of gelled water will stay somewhat wet.
