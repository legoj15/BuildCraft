# Robot Goggles

<ItemImage id="buildcraftunofficial:goggles" scale="2" float="right" />

Robots fly off to work and vanish behind walls, trees and hillsides, and you only learn a battery has run flat when one drops out of the sky. A redstone chipset set in the bridge of a pair of goggles shows you where every robot is and how much power it has left.

## Information

Wear them in the head slot, or right-click with them to put them on. With Curios installed they also fit its head slot, and with Accessories its face slot, so they can be worn alongside a helmet. They give no armour, never wear out and cannot be enchanted.

While they are on, every [Robot](./robot.md) in range is outlined through walls, trees and hillsides. The outline shows its charge: red when the battery is empty, green when it is full, and a dimmer blend of the two in between. A robot low enough to go looking for a charging station is already mostly red. Only you see the outlines; they are not part of the world.

They also outline the cells a stalled [Builder](../block/builder.md) is skipping, the same outlines a [Marker Wrench](./marker_wrench.md) or a [Volume Wrench](./volume_wrench.md) in your hand shows. Red marks a cell you have to fix yourself, such as a block that cannot stand where the blueprint puts it or one that cannot be broken. Yellow marks a cell waiting on supplies, and blue one held back by fluid.
