# Pipe Pulsar

<ItemImage id="buildcraftunofficial:plug_pulsar" scale="2" float="right" />

Powering your pipes for free without needing a big engine? How cool is that.

## Plug Mechanics

It can only be placed on a power-accepting pipe and can be right-clicked to be activated.

The activation of a Pipe Pulsar can also be done with a Gate using Pulsar actions.

- [Power Pulsar](../action/power_pulsar.md)
- [Single Power Pulse](../action/single_power_pulse.md)

## Pipe Connections

Pluggables can only be placed on pipes and can be placed on any side of a pipe.

It will stop any other pipe and pipe wire connection between two pipes when placed in between.
