# Robot

<ItemImage id="buildcraftunofficial:robot" scale="2" float="right" />

Five iron, a redstone crystal and two diamond chipsets: a body with nothing in its head. Until a board goes in it is just an expensive paperweight.

## Information

A blank robot cannot be placed: right-clicking a station with one shows "Not programmed" and keeps the item. Program it in the [Integration Table](../block/integration_table.md) with a board from the [Programming Table](../block/programming_table.md), then right-click a [Docking Station](./robot_station.md) on a pipe to put it to work. Blank robots stack to 16; programmed ones carry their board, charge and name, so they stack to 1.

The tooltip shows the board inside and the charge. A robot holds 10,000 MJ and goes looking for a charging station below 2,000 MJ; the Integration Table tops a drained robot up to that floor when it programs it.
