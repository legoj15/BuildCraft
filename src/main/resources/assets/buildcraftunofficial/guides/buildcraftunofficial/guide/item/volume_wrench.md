# Volume Wrench

<ItemImage id="buildcraftunofficial:volume_wrench" scale="2" float="right" />

Utilizing the power of chipsets, you can make a wrench that can edit existing volumes so that you don't need to start over anymore.

## Information

The Volume Wrench does everything the [Marker Wrench](./marker_wrench.md) does, while adding the ability to resize volumes created by [Land Mark](../block/marker_volume.md)s. This includes ones already attached to machines such as the [Filler](../block/filler.md) and [Architect Table](../block/architect.md).

To use, right-click one of the corner markers of a connected line, area, or volume, and aim where you want the new corner to be, it will change color while you are editing. Click again to keep the new size. Switch hands or crouch and right-click to cancel. Crouch + right-click a volume to remove it.

Size edits are limited to the currently configured range of marker connections. Changing a machine's area will cause it to restart its work if it was running.

This wrench lasts longer and has more durability than the previous iron-based ones. Editing volumes does not count against the durability.
