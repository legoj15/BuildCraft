# Volume Wrench

<ItemImage id="buildcraftunofficial:volume_wrench" scale="2" float="right" />

Marking out an area is only half the job. The other half is changing it once the machine is running.

## Information

The Volume Wrench does everything the [Marker Wrench](./marker_wrench.md) does, and it can also drag the corners of any area: the working area of a Filler or Architect Table that took over markers, or a floating box left behind by resizing markers.

Click the corner you want to move, then move where you are pointing and the area follows. Click again to keep the new size, or crouch and click to put it back the way it was. Put the wrench away mid-drag and the drag is cancelled.

It is made of diamond, so it lasts about six times as long as the other wrenches and is mended with diamonds, but only turning blocks, hitting and mining wear it: handling boxes costs nothing.

Edits are capped at the range markers can reach. Changing a machine's area restarts the machine's job, and the area goes away when the machine is broken.
