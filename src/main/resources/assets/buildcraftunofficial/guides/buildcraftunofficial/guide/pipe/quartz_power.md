## Pipe Mechanics

The Quartz Kinesis Pipe carries up to 32 MJ/t by default - enough for most mid-game machines - with the same modest loss as the other plain conduits.

Power is fed into the network by a Wooden Kinesis Pipe and delivered to any adjacent machine that consumes MJ.

- [Wooden Kinesis Pipe](./wood_power.md)

Quartz Kinesis Pipes will not connect to Cobblestone or Stone Kinesis Pipes.

Pipe Plugs, or painting pipes a different colour, can also stop pipes connecting.
