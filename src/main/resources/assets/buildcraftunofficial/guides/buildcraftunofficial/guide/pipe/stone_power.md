## Pipe Mechanics

The Stone Kinesis Pipe is a basic power conduit, carrying up to 8 MJ/t by default with modest loss - a step up from Cobblestone and a sensible choice for short runs.

Power is fed into the network by a Wooden Kinesis Pipe and delivered to any adjacent machine that consumes MJ.

- [Wooden Kinesis Pipe](./wood_power.md)

Stone Kinesis Pipes will not connect to Cobblestone or Quartz Kinesis Pipes.

Pipe Plugs, or painting pipes a different colour, can also stop pipes connecting.
