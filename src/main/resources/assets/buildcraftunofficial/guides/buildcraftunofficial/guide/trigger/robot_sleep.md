# Robot Sleep

Is the robot on this station asleep?

## Requirements

A robot goes to sleep on its home station when it has nothing to do or nothing to do it with, and wakes a minute later to look again. The trigger lets a gate notice that idle time: pair it with Wake Up, or with a pipe signal that tells another part of the base the robot is waiting.
